Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Vega AI is self-hosted software. This public website provides product information and does not provide app accounts or sign-in. When you run Vega AI, the person or organization operating that instance controls its deployment, data storage, access, and retention.

The self-hosted application

Data you enter into an instance is handled by that instance and its operator. The operator chooses where the app runs, who can access it, and how its data is backed up or deleted. Contact the operator of the instance you use with questions about its data practices.

Information that gets collected

When you use the browser extension

When you click the capture button on a LinkedIn job page, the extension collects the job information below and sends it to the Vega AI instance you configured:

  • Job title and company name
  • Job description and requirements
  • Location and job type (full-time, part-time, etc.)
  • The original job posting URL
  • Any notes you add before saving

The extension stores the credentials or authentication token needed to connect to your instance in your browser.

When you use the web application

Your instance stores the information you add directly or send through the extension. Depending on how the operator configured it, this may include:

  • Your account details, such as username and email address
  • Your profile information (skills, experience, education)
  • Application tracking data (which jobs you've applied to, interview dates, etc.)
  • Notes and status updates you add to jobs

Technical stuff

When you use an AI feature, the instance sends the relevant job and profile information to the AI provider configured by its operator. The provider's own data practices apply to those requests.

How the instance uses information

  • Job tracking: Store and organize your job applications
  • AI matching: Calculate how well jobs match your profile when you request an analysis
  • Document generation: Create tailored cover letters and resumes
  • Authentication: Keep you signed in across the extension and web app

Your control

  • Choose an instance: Select an operator and deployment you trust, or run your own
  • Manage your data: Ask your instance operator how to export or delete your data
  • Control AI usage: Use AI features only when needed, or configure a local model

Security

Instance operators are responsible for securing their deployment, including network access, encryption in transit, accounts, backups, and stored data. The extension keeps its connection credentials in your browser; protect access to that browser profile.

Data location

The instance operator chooses where the application and its data are hosted. If AI features are used, information sent for processing goes to the AI provider configured for that instance.

Open source

Both the browser extension and web application are open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can inspect the code, contribute improvements, or modify it for your own use. The source code is available on GitHub.

Third-party services

  • Public-site assets: The landing and privacy pages request fonts from Google Fonts and a Tailwind CSS script from its CDN. Those providers receive requests from your browser and may process request data under their own privacy policies.
  • Configured AI provider: Receives information needed for an AI feature when you use it; the provider is selected by the instance operator.

Intended use

Vega AI is designed for people organizing a job search. Instance operators are responsible for setting requirements for people who use their deployment.

Changes

This policy may be updated, and changes will be posted on this page. Check with your instance operator for changes to that instance's data practices.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy or how your data gets handled, email vega@benidevo.com or check out the code on GitHub .

Your rights

For data access, correction, export, or deletion requests, contact the operator of the instance that holds your information.
