Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Vega AI is self-hosted software. We don't run a Vega AI service, and this website has no accounts or sign-in. This page covers the website, the browser extension, and what to expect when you run Vega AI yourself.

This website

The site is static. It has no accounts, analytics, or ads, and sets no cookies of its own. Your browser still makes requests to the services below when you load a page, and they may log them under their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and sees standard request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts: serves the fonts.
  • Tailwind CDN: serves a CSS script.

The browser extension

When you click the capture button on a LinkedIn job page, the extension reads the job details and sends them to the Vega AI server address you set up:

  • Job title and company name
  • Job description and requirements
  • Location and job type (full-time, part-time, etc.)
  • The original job posting URL
  • Any notes you add before saving

This data goes only to your server. Nothing is sent to Vega AI or its maintainers. The extension has no analytics or error reporting, and we don't show ads or sell data.

The extension stores your server address and the token it uses to sign in in your browser. Protect access to that browser profile.

Two other requests happen: the extension checks GitHub for new releases, and it loads a font from Google Fonts.

Running Vega AI yourself

Your data stays on the machine where you run Vega AI. You decide where it is hosted, who can access it, how it is backed up, and when it is deleted. You are also responsible for securing it, including network access, accounts, and backups.

The one exception is AI features. When you use one, Vega AI sends the relevant job and profile text to the AI provider you configured, and that provider's privacy policy applies. If you configure a local model, nothing leaves your machine.

If you use a Vega AI server that someone else runs, that operator controls your data. Ask them how to export or delete it.

Open source

Both the browser extension and web application are open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can read the code on GitHub to check any claim on this page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
