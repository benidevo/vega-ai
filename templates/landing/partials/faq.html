Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How do I get started?

Run Vega AI with Docker on a computer or server you control, then configure an AI provider or local model. See the self-hosted quick start.

How does the job matching work?

Vega AI analyzes your skills and experience against job requirements to give you a match score. It looks at required skills, experience level, and other factors to help you focus on jobs where you have the best chances.

Can I use the browser extension with my instance?

Yes. Connect the extension to your self-hosted Vega AI instance and sign in with an account on that instance. It saves jobs from LinkedIn with one click and works with Chrome and Edge. Download the latest release.

Which AI models and providers are supported?

Vega AI works with any OpenAI-compatible provider. You can use:

  • Gemini (default): get an API key from Google AI Studio. Set AI_KEY=your-key.
  • OpenAI: GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, and others. Set AI_PROVIDER=openai and AI_KEY=sk-....
  • Ollama (local): run a model on your own hardware. Set AI_BASE_URL=http://host.docker.internal:11434/v1 and AI_KEY=ollama.

The configured model must support JSON mode. Smaller models may produce inconsistent results.

Is my data private?

Your instance operator controls where Vega AI runs and where its app data is stored. If you use AI features, the request goes to the AI provider configured for that instance.

What if I need help or find a bug?

The Vega AI community is active on GitHub. You can open issues, ask questions, or contribute improvements. Since it's open source, you can also modify it to fit your specific needs.

What does it cost to use Vega AI?

Vega AI is free and open source. You provide the computer or server that runs it and configure an AI provider; that provider may charge for its service. A local model can avoid per-request provider fees.
